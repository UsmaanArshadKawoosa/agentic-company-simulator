# Marketing System

## Marketing Funnel

```text
Awareness → Interest → Consideration → Conversion → Retention → Advocacy
```

The CMO manages this funnel through channels, campaigns, and messaging.

## Acquisition Model

Customer acquisition depends on:
- **Marketing effectiveness** (campaign quality × budget)
- **Product readiness** (product must exist to acquire users)
- **Market demand** (external market conditions)
- **Brand strength** (accumulated reputation)
- **Pricing competitiveness** (price vs market expectation)

### Acquisition Formula (Conceptual)
```text
acquisition_rate = base_rate × demand × product_factor × marketing_factor × brand_factor × price_factor
```

Where:
- `base_rate` = 5-20 new customers per step (varies by segment)
- `product_factor` = product_readiness (0–1)
- `marketing_factor` = campaign effectiveness × budget efficiency (0–1)
- `brand_factor` = brand_strength (0.1–1.0)
- `price_factor` = 1.0 if competitive, 0.7 if overpriced, 1.1 if underpriced

## Campaigns

### Campaign Lifecycle
1. **Plan**: Define target segment, message, budget, duration, success metric (CAC).
2. **Execute**: Spend budget over duration steps.
3. **Measure**: Track CAC, conversion rate, funnel metrics.
4. **Optimize**: Adjust message, targeting, or budget.
5. **Scale/Sunset**: Increase budget on winners, stop losers.

### Campaign Templates
- **Launch campaign**: High budget, broad awareness, short duration.
- **Growth campaign**: Medium budget, targeted, long duration.
- **Retention campaign**: Low budget, existing users, focus on engagement.
- **Content campaign**: Low budget, compounding, long-term value.

### Campaign Budget
- Small campaign: 5-10% of monthly burn.
- Medium campaign: 15-25% of monthly burn.
- Large campaign: 30-50% of monthly burn.

## Brand Strength

- Starts at 0.1 (new company).
- Increases with successful campaigns, customer satisfaction, press coverage.
- Decreases with negative events, quality issues, competitor actions.
- Affects all future acquisition rates.

## Marketing Constraints

- Cannot spend more than available cash.
- Cannot spend more than approved budget authority.
- Campaigns take 1-2 steps to show results (lead time).
- Poor product quality reduces campaign effectiveness.
