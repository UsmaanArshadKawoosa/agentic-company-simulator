# Fundraising System

## Fundraising Pipeline

Fundraising is a process, not an event. The company moves through stages:

```text
Discover → Contact → Interested → Due Diligence → Offer → Invested
```

### Pipeline Stages
| Stage | Probability of success | Notes |
|-------|----------------------|-------|
| Discover | — | Identify target investors |
| Contact | 30% | Initial outreach |
| Interested | 40% | Basic interest shown |
| Due Diligence | 60% | Share financials, metrics |
| Offer | 80% | Term sheet negotiation |
| Invested | — | Funds received |

## Investor Profiles

Investors evaluate:
- **Traction**: revenue, user growth, retention
- **Team**: experience, track record
- **Market**: size, growth, competition
- **Financials**: burn rate, runway, unit economics
- **Product**: quality, differentiation

### Investor Types
| Type | Check size | Stage focus |
|------|-----------|-------------|
| Friends & Family | $10K–$100K | Idea, Prototype |
| Angel | $25K–$500K | MVP, Early traction |
| Seed fund | $500K–$2M | Product-market fit |
| Series A | $2M–$15M | Scale, revenue |
| Growth equity | $15M+ | Mature, profitable |

## Fundraising Process

### Per Step
1. Review current pipeline (investors at each stage).
2. Advance investors based on momentum and metrics.
3. Generate new investor prospects.
4. Handle due diligence requests.
5. Negotiate terms on offers.

### Success Factors
- Strong metrics improve conversion rates.
- Poor metrics stall fundraising.
- Each fundraising attempt takes 1-2 steps of CEO time.
- Failed attempts create negative signal.

## Valuation

### Pre-money Valuation
```text
pre_money = base_valuation + 
    (revenue × revenue_multiple) +
    (users × user_multiple) +
    (team_quality × team_multiplier) -
    (risks × risk_discount)
```

### Key Metrics
- **Revenue multiple**: 3-10x annual recurring revenue
- **User multiple**: $0.50-5.00 per active user
- **Burn multiple**: valuation / (monthly burn × runway months)

## Fundraising Constraints

- Cannot raise more than market condition supports.
- Over-valuation leads to down round risk.
- Under-valuation wastes equity.
- Fundraising takes 2-4 steps to complete.
- Cannot raise while failing (cash exhaustion, CRITICAL health).

## Dilution

Funding rounds dilute existing shareholders:
- Friends & Family: 10-20%
- Seed: 15-25%
- Series A: 20-30%
- Series B+: 15-25%

Dilution is tracked in the cap table.
