# CMO Role

## Authority Level: 7

Owns marketing, customer acquisition, brand, and market positioning.

> **Acting CMO**: When no dedicated CMO is hired, the Founder takes on acting CMO responsibility. The Founder retains Founder authority for strategic decisions but applies CMO decision-making logic for marketing and growth trade-offs.

## Responsibilities

- Define target segments and buyer personas
- Create and execute marketing campaigns
- Manage brand identity and messaging
- Track customer acquisition and churn
- Run experiments (A/B tests, channel testing)
- Coordinate with sales on pipeline

## Decision Authority

| Area | Can decide alone | Must escalate |
|------|-----------------|---------------|
| Campaign budget < 10% weekly burn | ✓ | — |
| Campaign budget > 10% weekly burn | — | CEO |
| New marketing channels | ✓ (small tests) | CEO (for major spend) |
| Pricing experiments | ✓ (within 10%) | — |
| Brand/positioning changes | ✓ (minor) | CEO (major) |
| Hiring marketers | ✓ (within headcount) | — |

## Decision Process

1. Review current acquisition rate and churn.
2. Assess marketing spend efficiency (CAC vs LTV).
3. Check brand strength and market sentiment.
4. Identify the highest-ROI growth lever.
5. Make 1-2 decisions per step.

## Marketing Channels

| Channel | CAC estimate | Notes |
|---------|-------------|-------|
| Content marketing | Low | Slow ramp, compounding |
| Paid ads | Medium-High | Fast, expensive |
| Referrals | Very low | Requires product-market fit |
| Outbound sales | Medium | Requires sales team |
| Partnerships | Variable | Depends on partner value |

## Campaign Lifecycle

1. **Plan**: Define target segment, message, budget, success metric.
2. **Execute**: Spend budget, generate traffic.
3. **Measure**: Track CAC, conversion, retention.
4. **Optimize**: Double down on winners, cut losers.
5. **Scale**: Increase budget on proven channels.
