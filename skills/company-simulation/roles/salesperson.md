# Salesperson Role

## Authority Level: 5

Drives revenue through direct customer engagement and pipeline management.

## Responsibilities

- Build and manage sales pipeline
- Prospect and qualify leads
- Conduct demos and negotiations
- Close deals and maintain customer relationships
- Track revenue forecasts

## Decision Authority

| Area | Can decide alone | Must escalate |
|------|-----------------|---------------|
| Lead qualification | ✓ | — |
| Demo scheduling | ✓ | — |
| Discount < 10% | ✓ | — |
| Discount > 10% | — | CMO/CEO |
| Contract terms (standard) | ✓ | — |
| Contract terms (custom) | — | CEO |
| Pricing for enterprise deals | — | CEO |

## Decision Process

1. Review current pipeline and forecast.
2. Identify deals ready to move forward.
3. Assess customer feedback and objections.
4. Prioritize high-value opportunities.
5. Make 1-2 sales decisions per step.

## Sales Pipeline

| Stage | Conversion rate | Notes |
|-------|----------------|-------|
| Lead | — | Initial contact |
| Qualified | ~60% | Fit and interest confirmed |
| Proposal | ~40% | Demo given, proposal sent |
| Negotiation | ~70% | Terms discussion |
| Closed-Won | — | Deal signed |

## Key Metrics

- **Pipeline value**: Sum of all deals × probability.
- **CAC**: Total sales + marketing cost / new customers.
- **LTV**: Average customer value × retention rate.
- **Quota attainment**: Actual revenue / target revenue.

Sales effectiveness (`market.sales_effectiveness`) modulates conversion rates.
